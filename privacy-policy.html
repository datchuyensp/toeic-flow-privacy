VocaUp Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 16, 2026 · Last updated: November 25, 2026

Delete account · Delete learning data · Privacy contact

1. About this policy

This policy explains how VocaUp, formerly named TOEIC Flow, handles information. The VocaUp developer can be contacted at datchuyensp@gmail.com. The app provides English-learning activities. You can sign in with a supported provider or continue as a guest. Guest mode creates a VocaUp account tied to your app installation.

Summary: VocaUp stores and synchronizes learning progress, profile information and purchases. We also use service providers for sign-in, analytics, advertising and payments. To exercise your deletion options, see Delete your account and Delete learning data.

2. Accounts and learning data

VocaUp uses account and installation identifiers to establish and protect sessions, synchronize learning progress and apply device-based trial limits. When you sign in with Google or, on iOS, Apple, we receive information from that provider to verify your identity. This can include an identity token, a provider identifier, and an email address or profile information when available. When you edit your profile, we receive the display name and profile picture you provide. We store account-linked learning progress, practice results and related preferences on our servers for synchronization. The app also keeps local copies and settings for supported offline features. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the app removes local copies but does not request deletion of server records.

Some learning interactions and progress-related events are also sent as analytics events as described below; local storage of detailed progress does not mean that all activity information stays on your device.

3. Usage analytics

The Android app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand feature usage and improve the learning experience. Events can include screens and features used, learning milestones, practice interactions, and purchase-flow actions or outcomes. The Firebase SDK can also process app-instance identifiers, device and operating-system information, app version, session information and approximate geographic information derived from network data. Advertising identifiers may be processed depending on SDK configuration, device settings and applicable permissions.

Analytics information is transmitted to Google. We use analytics reports to understand engagement, identify problems and improve the app. We do not intentionally include names, email addresses or phone numbers in our custom analytics events. The current app does not provide a dedicated in-app analytics opt-out switch; device advertising controls do not necessarily disable analytics collection.

4. Advertising and consent choices

The app integrates Google Mobile Ads for optional rewarded advertisements. Depending on the build, advertisements may be test ads rather than live ads. The advertising and consent SDKs can process IP addresses, device or advertising identifiers, app interactions, ad views and responses, diagnostic information and consent choices to deliver ads, measure performance, prevent fraud and comply with applicable requirements. Non-personalized or test ads should not be assumed to involve no data processing.

The app requests advertising consent information using Google's consent tools before requesting ads. Where supported and required, a consent form or privacy options form allows you to manage applicable advertising choices. You may also use your device's advertising privacy controls. Availability and effects of these controls depend on your region, device and the relevant service. You can choose not to watch rewarded advertisements. Premium provides an ad-free learning experience but does not automatically disable usage analytics.

5. Purchases and subscription information

Google Play processes Android purchases and payments. Apple handles purchases and payments made through the iOS app. The app receives information needed to verify, restore and manage access, such as product identifiers, purchase identifiers or tokens, transaction status, acknowledgement status and subscription validity information. VocaUp servers verify purchases and maintain wallet and Premium entitlement records. The app caches access information locally. Google and Apple maintain their own store purchase records.

VocaUp does not receive your full payment card number through in-app billing. Purchase-flow analytics may record actions and outcomes; this is separate from Google's processing of your payment. See our Terms of Service for renewal, cancellation and refund information.

6. Support communications and device features

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to provide, such as a problem description, screenshots or an order ID. We use these details to respond and resolve your request. Do not send passwords, one-time security codes or full card details.

The app uses audio playback, text-to-speech and vibration for learning, and may request notification permission for study reminders. Optional speaking and conversation features use microphone and speech recognition permissions. The device speech service may process audio on the device or send it to its provider, depending on language, settings and availability. The standalone conversation translator uses downloaded on-device translation models. Talk to Voca sends conversation text, including recognized speech used as input, to VocaUp servers and may pass it to an AI service to generate a reply. Talk conversations are stored with your account. VocaUp does not request contacts or precise device location. Text-to-speech processing may depend on the speech engine and voice selected on your device.

7. Service providers and disclosures

Information is processed by sign-in, hosting, analytics, advertising, consent, AI, payment and support providers as described above. The learning app also embeds videos from YouTube. YouTube may receive information when you load or interact with embedded content under Google’s policies. We do not sell your personal information for money. Advertising-related processing may be subject to additional definitions and rights under local privacy law; this policy does not waive those rights.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect users, prevent fraud or defend legal rights. Relevant provider information is available at:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase privacy and security information
  • Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosures
  • How Google uses information from apps and sites using its services

8. Request deletion of your VocaUp account

Without the app: Email datchuyensp@gmail.com with the subject VocaUp account deletion request. Include the email linked to your VocaUp account, if you have one, or mention that you used a guest account. We may ask for information needed to locate the account and verify the request. Please do not send passwords or security codes. You can submit the request by email even if you have uninstalled VocaUp.

In the app: Open Tài khoản → Xóa tài khoản, enter DELETE when prompted and complete confirmation. VocaUp may ask you to sign in again to verify your identity.

Deleted or anonymized: We revoke VocaUp sessions, remove sign-in links and device associations, delete the account’s learning state, preferences, Talk conversations and other account-linked feature records, and anonymize profile identifiers. The app clears the account’s local session and learning cache after deletion.

Records retained: Limited, anonymized V-Coin ledger and purchase records and a non-identifying deletion record may be retained for seven years under our financial retention policy, or longer where needed for a legal hold or applicable obligations. These records support accounting, refunds, dispute resolution, fraud prevention and eligible purchase restoration. Backups expire according to their rotation schedule. Google and Apple handle their own account, ad, analytics and purchase records under their policies. Deleting VocaUp does not cancel a store subscription. Cancel it separately in Google Play or Apple account settings.

Delete VocaUp learning data and keep your account

In VocaUp, open Tài khoản → Đặt lại dữ liệu học and confirm. This clears current learning progress and practice state in the app. For accounts that sync, the reset is sent to our servers when synchronization completes. Account details, purchases, wallet records and Talk conversations are outside this reset. Backups may retain earlier copies until rotation. For help requesting a learning-data reset, email datchuyensp@gmail.com with the subject VocaUp learning data deletion request and specify that you want to keep your account.

You may also email us to request access, correction or deletion of information we control, or to exercise other applicable privacy rights. We retain support correspondence as needed to handle requests, resolve disputes and meet applicable legal obligations. Analytics and advertising retention follows the applicable service configurations and provider policies.

9. Security and international processing

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information handled, but no storage or transmission method is completely secure. Local data protection also depends on your device security. Keep your operating system updated and protect access to your device.

Google and other service providers may process information in countries other than your own under their applicable privacy terms and safeguards. Where local law requires a legal basis, notice or consent for processing, those requirements remain applicable; publishing this policy alone is not a substitute for obtaining required consent.

10. Children's privacy

VocaUp is an English-learning and exam-preparation tool and is not designed primarily for children under 13. We do not knowingly seek personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so that we can investigate and take appropriate action. A parent or guardian should supervise use and purchases where required by local law.

11. Policy updates

We may update this policy when features, data practices or legal requirements change. The latest revision date appears above. Material changes will be communicated appropriately, and additional consent will be requested where required. This revision adds current information about VocaUp accounts, learning synchronization and data deletion.
